How it plays

One stick of input. Every second of it matters.

Crank to aim

The beam is always on, so aiming is really choosing what you’re not shooting. Turn too far and something reaches the core behind you.

Shoot mods to build a run

Amplify, Spread, Echo, Pulse, Nova Shield, Stasis Shield — three levels each, stacking into a build you didn’t plan and can’t repeat.

Don’t shoot the glitches

Glitch tokens sit in the same lane as the good ones. Hit one and you get Dampen, Drag, Narrow, Trim, Void or Burst — weaker laser, slower turn, upgrades wiped.
